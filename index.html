Flippy Mat: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Flippy Mat ("the app") is made by DenBroTech ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your scores, the number of games you've played, the beer mats you've won and your settings (sound, music and so on) are saved on your phone only. We don't have a server, and we don't receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google Play Games (if you use it)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores can be sent to a Google Play Games leaderboard, where they're shown with your Play Games profile name. This is handled by Google under its privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a score card or a replay video, the app makes a picture or video on your phone and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

What we don't do

We don't ask you to create an account, we don't collect your name, email or contacts, we don't use your microphone, camera or precise location, and we don't sell any information.

Age

Flippy Mat is set in a pub and is meant for adults. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
